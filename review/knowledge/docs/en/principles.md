# Principles and baseline

The common minimum and the space for local decisions.

## Purpose

A policy matters when someone can act on it. KDV connects what you want to govern, who can decide, how work flows and what demonstrates that it works.

## Minimum baseline

Every shared product needs ownership, a contract, a classification, policy-based access and an audit trail. Each ecosystem adapts role names, approval routes, thresholds and review cadences around that minimum.

- Named ownership
- Versioned contract
- Classification
- Access by policy
- Auditability

## Local adaptation

Keep the baseline visible while adapting names, review routes, thresholds and delivery tools. Record the scope, approver and reason for an exception. A local variation should not silently remove ownership or make a decision untraceable.

## Five operating layers

### Principles & policies

What must hold true. Purpose, classification, retention and the minimum governance baseline.

### Organisation & roles

Who decides and delivers. Explicit owners and stewards, with responsibilities assigned at the right scope.

### Processes & decisions

How work moves. Cultivate, govern, share and activate, with review and exception paths.

### Controls & enablers

How rules take effect. Contracts, quality checks, access policies, catalogues and operational safeguards.

### Evidence & improvement

How we know and learn. Decision records, test results, usage, incidents and agreed outcome measures.
