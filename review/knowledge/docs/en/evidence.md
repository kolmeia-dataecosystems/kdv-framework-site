# Evidence and improvement

Observe execution and outcomes without inventing maturity scores.

## Purpose

State the population, time window, definition and accountable reviewer for each indicator. A missing test result is missing evidence, not a pass. Look at trends, exceptions and fitness for purpose. The checklist is a discussion aid; these measures do not produce an official DCAM score or an asserted equivalence to a maturity model.

## Evidence and review

- Ownership coverage: products with named owner / products in scope.
- Contract coverage: shared products with a current agreement / shared products in scope.
- Quality adherence: successful evaluated checks / evaluated checks, with sampling and period stated.
- Access lead time: elapsed time from a complete request to an implemented decision; record exclusions.
- Value: a use-case-specific outcome agreed with the consumer, not a count of assets alone.

## Suggested review rhythm

### At each publication

Review contract, ownership, quality and access terms before release.

### Weekly, as a starting point

Triage issues, access requests and contract changes with the delivery team.

### Monthly, as a starting point

Review outcomes, exceptions and policy improvements with the governance forum.

## Review questions

- Who can decide, and is the acting role explicit?
- What rule or agreement authorises the action?
- What evidence demonstrates execution and its limitations?
- When will the outcome or exception be reviewed?

## Define the direction of every indicator

Record unit, population, observation period, comparator and target. For freshness or access lead time, lower may be better; for completeness, higher may be better. A blank observation is missing evidence, not zero or success. Separate evidence of execution from evidence of effectiveness; technical logs alone do not establish maturity.
