# Data product agreements

Make ownership, quality, compatibility and use explicit.

## Purpose

A contract is a versioned working agreement between the people producing, governing and consuming a product. It describes what is offered, to whom, for which purposes and with which expectations. A schema alone does not establish ownership or permission. The original KDV template is human-readable and does not claim conformance to ODCS or another technical standard.

## Minimum baseline

- Identity, version, Hive, Cell and product owner
- Purpose, intended consumers and delivery interfaces
- Definitions, schema, provenance and lineage
- Classification, permitted use, retention and access conditions
- Quality thresholds, tests, freshness and incident contact
- Compatibility, deprecation period and review/approval record

## Version and compatibility

Agree how to distinguish breaking changes, compatible additions and documentation corrections. Notify consumers before a breaking version replaces their agreement. Keep previous approvals and a planned transition or retirement path. Semantic versioning can be adopted, but the team must define what a change means for its consumers.

## Synthetic example

A fictional service-planning team needs a weekly aggregate of requests. The service domain owns the meaning; a subdomain team prepares the data; a product owner agrees freshness and allowed use. The first release is an internal aggregate, not a copy of identifiable source records. The team records quality results and reviews whether the product supports the intended planning decision.

## Review questions

- Who can decide, and is the acting role explicit?
- What rule or agreement authorises the action?
- What evidence demonstrates execution and its limitations?
- When will the outcome or exception be reviewed?

## Privacy and permitted AI use

Record purpose, applicable legal roles, minimised inputs, retention, access review, allowed AI use and conditions for new consumers. The DPO advises; the accountable organisation retains its legal responsibilities. Link the approval to the policy actually implemented.
