# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of private information. Make room for different languages, experiences and organisational contexts. Give proper credit and avoid unsupported claims of certification or endorsement.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, request corrections, close discussions or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors. Reports can be sent to info@kolmeia.io. Explain the incident without redistributing personal data unnecessarily. Maintainers will handle reports with appropriate discretion and may need relevant context to act.
