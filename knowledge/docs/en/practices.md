# Practices and references

An original operational interpretation of the three reference lenses.

## Purpose

An original KDV interpretation for operational use. These connections are illustrative, not an official equivalence, assessment or reproduction of the source frameworks.

## Working sequence

| Reference | Practice lens | KDV operational expression | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DAMA-DMBOK 2 | Governance and accountability | Named owners and scoped decision rights. | Role register and decision record. |
| DAMA-DMBOK 2 | Metadata and quality | Shared definitions, product context and quality rules. | Glossary, lineage and test results. |
| DAMA-DMBOK 2 | Security and operations | Classification, authorised access and recovery practices. | Policy record and recovery check. |
| DCAM | Capability development | Agree priorities, assign responsibilities and review improvements. | Action backlog and accountable follow-up. |
| DCAM | Evidence of practice | Demonstrate execution and outcomes over time. | Decisions, controls and indicator trends. |
| Data Mesh | Domain ownership | Hives and Cells own their products and meaning. | Domain and product charters. |
| Data Mesh | Data as a product | Versioned contracts and consumer-oriented delivery. | Contract, feedback and usage. |
| Data Mesh | Self-serve infrastructure | Reusable delivery paths with clear operating boundaries. | Service interface and operational checks. |
| Data Mesh | Federated computational governance | Common rules, local decisions and automated checks where feasible. | Baseline, policy tests and exceptions. |

## Original references

- [DAMA-DMBOK 2](https://dama.org/dama-dmbok2-revised-edition-faqs/): The data management knowledge reference informing the governance vocabulary.
- [DCAM](https://edmcouncil.org/frameworks/dcam/): A capability framework informing evidence-led improvement. Consult its publisher for access and licensing.
- [Data Mesh](https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html): The four principles informing domain ownership, data products, self-service and federation.

## Scope and limitations

KDV adds an original operating interpretation. DAMA-DMBOK and DCAM material is not redistributed. References do not imply endorsement, affiliation, certification or an official mapping.
