# Access and exceptions

Follow a request from intended purpose to review and expiry.

## Purpose

Review the requested purpose against classification and the current contract. The steward prepares the assessment; the authorised owner decides. A technical custodian implements the approved policy. Approval and implementation are separate acts, and both need evidence. Denial and escalation should carry a reason rather than an unexplained status.

## Working sequence

1. **Consumer:** Requests a version and states the intended use.
2. **Steward:** Checks purpose, meaning, classification and fit.
3. **Owner:** Approves, rejects or escalates with a recorded reason.
4. **Custodian:** Implements the authorised policy and verifies access.
5. **Consumer + owner:** Review use, expiry, exceptions and continued need.

## Local adaptation

An exception records the affected rule, business reason, risk, accountable approver, compensating control and expiry or review date. An exception is not a permanent shortcut around the baseline. Revoke access when its authorised purpose or period ends and retain an appropriate decision trail.

## Review questions

- Who can decide, and is the acting role explicit?
- What rule or agreement authorises the action?
- What evidence demonstrates execution and its limitations?
- When will the outcome or exception be reviewed?
