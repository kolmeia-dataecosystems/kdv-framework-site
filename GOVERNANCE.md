# Stewardship of the open community framework

KDV Ecosystems initiates and maintains this edition. It is a starting operating model open for review, not a declaration of an existing independent standards body or established community consensus.

- Everyone may propose a correction, use case or improvement through repository issues and pull requests.
- Repository maintainers appointed by KDV review changes. Community review is welcome, particularly from people affected by the proposed practice.
- Decisions record the problem, options, rationale and implications. Meaningful conceptual changes update `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Core vocabulary and responsibility changes must explain compatibility with existing templates and diagrams. The open model stays distinct from any specific implementation's feature availability.
- Disagreements should be discussed against use cases, evidence and limitations. Maintainers document their decision in the issue or PR; a proposal can be revisited with new evidence.
- A release is a versioned community artefact. The original edition 0.1 is preserved as the v1.0.0 baseline; later versions follow [the release policy](docs/RELEASING.md). No DCAM/DMBOK equivalence or assessment authority is claimed.

The five-part baseline is named ownership, versioned agreement, classification, authorised access and auditability. Ecosystems applying the model may extend it and adapt roles, language and decision paths to their context while retaining explicit responsibility.
